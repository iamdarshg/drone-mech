# Fresh early-time localization runs

This paired fresh-from-zero run tests the existing maxCo=0.2 case at IAS700 and IAS900 with output every 10 time steps (the previous controls wrote every 100). The higher write frequency is the only change from the co020 setup; it is intended to resolve early checkpoint evolution. Both runs were capped at 180 wall seconds with a target end time of 25 microseconds. They exited at the wall cap (124), without an `End` marker. The latest **complete** checkpoints analyzed were IAS700 15.334 microseconds and IAS900 18.188 microseconds; an incomplete/partially written final state is excluded.

At about 10 microseconds, IAS700 has T=223.6–298.7 K, maximum speed 258.7 m/s (cell 46164), and maximum pressure 88.8 kPa (cell 45925). IAS900 has T=220.1–284.0 K, maximum speed 323.8 m/s (cell 46164), and maximum pressure 94.5 kPa (cell 45925). At the final complete checkpoints, maximum speed/T are 269.2 m/s/286.9 K for IAS700 and 328.7 m/s/292.3 K for IAS900. These are early flow-development observations only. The late excursion documented by the previous co020 runs occurs around 0.12–0.15 ms, which these short runs do not reach; they therefore do not localize its onset.

`checkMesh -allTopology -allGeometry` reports `Mesh OK`, but maximum non-orthogonality is 83.464 degrees and maximum skewness is 2.774. An isolated `checkMesh -writeAllFields` diagnostic was used to compare cell-quality fields with solution hotspot cell IDs. Early pressure/speed extrema include cells 44451 (non-orthogonality 69.20°), 46164 (82.96°), and 49177 (82.34°). In the prior late failing history, cells 42158 and 42335 had non-orthogonality 29.06° and 18.79° while showing extreme speed/temperature. The eventual excursion therefore does not map only to the mesh's worst non-orthogonality cells; the actual cause remains unresolved.

Evidence: `logs/ias700.log.gz`, `logs/ias900.log.gz`, `checkpoint_history_ias700.json`, `checkpoint_history_ias900.json`, and `logs/checkMesh_writeAllFields.log.gz`. The compressed `case_bundle_ias*.tar.gz` archives retain t=0, the checkpoint nearest 10 microseconds, and the latest complete OpenFOAM checkpoint with native field files; all per-write checkpoint extrema remain in the JSON histories. `run_manifest.json` in each case records input hashes, source hashes, solver identity, cap, exit and checkpoint times.
