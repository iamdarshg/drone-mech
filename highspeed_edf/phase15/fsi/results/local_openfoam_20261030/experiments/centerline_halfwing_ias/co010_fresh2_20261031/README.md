# Fresh maxCo=0.1 transient continuation check

This paired local OpenFOAM 1912 experiment restarts the same rigid, centerline half-wing cases from t=0 at IAS 700 and 900 km/h. Compared with `co020/long_detached_20261031`, it changes `maxCo` from 0.2 to 0.1 and changes output retention only (write every 50 steps, retain 3 checkpoints). Geometry, mesh, initial fields, boundary conditions, SST model, PIMPLE/transonic formulation, pressure solver, and spatial schemes are source-pinned in each `run_manifest.json`. Solver binary SHA256: `925288f3e89e39b06f66e401542c5985eea0a538e9f9d781f1ef09c39155c586`; source commit: `86d8385206e9a213fa9e1fe66e506d8bb1477cbb`.

Both were capped at 1800 s and stopped by the monitor once saved fields crossed its broad runaway thresholds (T>1000 K or speed>1000 m/s). IAS700 stopped at 138.11 us with Tmax=1128.7 K, Umax=1088.3 m/s and pmax=205.5 kPa. IAS900 stopped at 120.78 us with Tmax=707.6 K, Umax=1252.5 m/s and pmax=175.3 kPa. Neither reached 0.15 ms. The latest persisted values are in `checkpoint_history_ias{700,900}.json`; `monitor.log`, `status_ias*.txt`, and solver logs preserve termination and run history.

The halved maxCo did not remove the rapid leading-edge pressure/temperature/speed growth seen in the same coarse grid; it only changed transient timing somewhat. At IAS700, the stored trajectory rose from (t=128.48 us, Tmax=697 K, Umax=606 m/s, pmax=170.7 kPa) to the guard stop over 9.64 us. At IAS900, it rose from (113.68 us, 584 K, 728 m/s, 162.9 kPa) to the guard stop over 7.11 us. The prior co0.2 paired runs show the same qualitative runaway, so this is not evidence of a stable or validated flow.

At the final persisted checkpoints the open-boundary mass imbalance is about 0.005% of throughflow (net 0.00846 kg/s at IAS700 against roughly 170 kg/s and 0.01066 kg/s at IAS900 against roughly 216 kg/s). This conservation check does not make the local field physically credible. No convergence or grid/time sensitivity result is established.
