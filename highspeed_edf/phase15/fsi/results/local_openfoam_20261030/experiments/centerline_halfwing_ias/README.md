# Experimental half-wing with symmetry at the centerline

This bounded sensitivity experiment extends the source generator's NACA-like, linear chord/sweep schedule from its original x=80.33 mm cut to x=0, preserving the 30 mm section thickness and 25 degree sweep. It places the symmetry plane at x=0, where a mirror half-wing is physically symmetric. It does not include a fuselage, wing-root fillet, EDF flow, or the as-designed inboard wing geometry. The extension is an isolated solver experiment; production geometry and the tracked source mesh are unchanged.

The centerline section has 425 mm chord and the tip section has 305 mm chord; the wing extends from x=0 to 260 mm. The generated STEP reopens as one positive-volume solid (0.001947276945 m³), with bounds x=0..0.260 m, y≈−0.0152..0.0149 m, z≈−0.270..0.1562 m. The fluid mesh has 49,208 tetrahedra, one connected region, and named inlet, outlet, farfield, fsi, and rootWall patches. `rootWall` is explicitly `symmetryPlane` at x=0 and `fsi` is a stationary wall.

The mesh passes OpenFOAM 1912 `checkMesh`, but quality remains mixed: maximum non-orthogonality 83.46 degrees, average 22.36 degrees, 83 faces above 70 degrees, maximum skewness 2.77. This mesh is larger than the 34,166-cell outboard-sector mesh and has worse average non-orthogonality/skewness. It is therefore a boundary-placement experiment, not a mesh-quality or mesh-convergence improvement.

IAS700 and IAS900 cases use clean `PIMPLE transonic yes`, PBiCGStab/DILU pressure, exactly three non-orthogonal corrections verified by `foamDictionary`, and limited corrected Laplacians. They start from the source t=0 fields, use the same original farfield distances and boundary types, and are capped at 600 s. Native `checkMesh` and `rhoPimpleFoam -dry-run` pass for each. Solver outcome, checkpoints, mass flow, and pressure-only load history will be appended after the bounded runs finish. These cases cannot establish full-aircraft aerodynamics or final forces because the fuselage, actual wing-root attachment, viscous load history, and mesh/time-step sensitivity are absent.

`generate_centerline_mesh.py` modifies only the root span station in a copy of the Phase-15 source generator's procedure; `prepare_centerline_cases.py` performs the native `gmshToFoam` import, patch typing, checkMesh, and case preparation. Gmsh Python binding 4.15.2 with bundled runtime 4.13.1 was used; Netgen optimization is disabled because the pinned runtime does not provide it. Input and generated output hashes are in `mesh_provenance.json`.

## Bounded transient outcome

Native `rhoPimpleFoam` was run fresh from the original t=0 fields for 600 s wall-clock cap on each speed. The unmodified logs and shell exit codes are in `logs/`. Both runs ended before the cap with exit 136 (floating-point exception) inside `hePsiThermo::calculate`, not with `End` or a completed simulation. IAS700 reached t=1.61770e-4 s and IAS900 reached t≈1.49121e-4 s. The respective maximum Courant numbers immediately before failure were about 0.39–0.40; the last complete checkpoint may be earlier because output is written every 100 steps.

The checkpoint analyzer at `cases/checkpoint_history_ias700.json` and `cases/checkpoint_history_ias900.json` reports only persisted field states, not the failing in-memory state. The most recent persisted checkpoints are IAS700 t=1.33934e-4 s and IAS900 t=1.33834e-4 s. At those times IAS700 had T=173–588 K and maximum speed 460 m/s near x=0.0175 m at the wing; IAS900 had T=177–747 K and maximum speed 1,549 m/s near x=0.189 m. The extreme local speeds/temperatures indicate a developing nonphysical solution before failure. These locations do not identify the exact failing cell. The root cut is no longer the sole obvious hotspot: the IAS900 maximum was outboard, while IAS700's was close to the centerline section.

At these saved states, integrated inlet/outlet mass flow was about 170 kg/s (IAS700) and 217 kg/s (IAS900), with net open-boundary imbalance 0.0081 and 0.0099 kg/s, respectively (about 0.005%). Pressure-only integrated loads were approximately [655, −69, −125] N and [677, −101, −181] N in global XYZ. These are transient diagnostics only; they omit viscous stress, include the experimental inboard geometry, and are not design loads.

Using ISA density at 3 km to convert IAS to an approximate TAS gives chord flow-through times of roughly 1.9 ms (IAS700) and 1.5 ms (IAS900) for the 425 mm root chord. The completed simulated time is only about 0.08–0.10 of that estimate. No steady-state or convergence claim is supported. A follow-up fresh-from-zero maxCo=0.2 experiment is recorded separately under `co020/` to test time-step sensitivity.
